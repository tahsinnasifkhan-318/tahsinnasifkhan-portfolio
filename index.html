Clothing Shop Order Management Automation

A demo workflow for clothing shops. The shop owner enters order details into a Google Sheet; Make.com checks quantity and updates the status.

  • Valid quantity → Pending Human Approval
  • Invalid quantity → Needs Correction
  • Owner reviews before confirming an order
